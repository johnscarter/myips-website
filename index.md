# MyIPS

## Take control of your health info

MyIPS is a mobile app that lets you create, maintain, and share a personal health summary based on the [International Patient Summary (IPS)](https://international-patient-summary.net/) standard and the HL7® FHIR® standard R4 specifications. The summary can be read by clinical systems around the world, making it useful in emergencies, when travelling, or when moving between healthcare providers. All data is stored in an encrypted database on your device — there is no account, no server, and nothing is transmitted unless you explicitly share it.

## Why MyIPS?

The health system generates heaps of electronic data about your care. But this valuable health info may be scattered across settings, it's usually controlled by someone else (such as health providers, payers, or government agencies), and it may be incorrect or incomplete. It's hard to bring all that info into one place, correct or clarify it, and add your own perspective.

It's also hard to share critical health info with caregivers. We fill out endless intake forms, we repeat our health concerns and previous treatment to each new staff member. In an emergency when we can't speak for ourselves, we only hope a health worker can find the important info they need to help us.

## The MyIPS Solution

MyIPS helps you take control of your health info.

- MyIPS lets you enter your own health summary data, organized into topical sections defined by the International Patient Summary (IPS).
- MyIPS lets you import and integrate data from your health providers using the FHIR® standard defined by HL7 International.
- MyIPS lets you choose when and how to share your info, again using a FHIR® standard format that many providers worldwide can understand.
- MyIPS is not a full-size medical record, but it helps you build and manage a health summary with exactly the information you choose.

## Features

- **Privacy and Security**
  - **Local-only storage** — no account required, no data sent to any server, no telemetry
  - **Provenance tracking** — every change is recorded with FHIR® Provenance metadata
- **Easy Data Entry**
  - **Medications** — record current and past medications with dosage and status
  - **Allergies and intolerances** — document allergic reactions and intolerances with severity and clinical status
  - **Health concerns** — record diagnosed conditions and health problems
  - **Immunisations** — log vaccination history
  - **Lab results** — store key laboratory findings
  - **Patient story** — add narrative context about your health history in your own words
- **Data Integration**
  - **Import data from providers** — import FHIR-formatted data from many sources
  - **Flag and remove duplicate items**
  - **Annotate, update or correct your health info**
- **Share Your Summary**
  - **Export as PDF** — share a human-readable summary via your device's share sheet
  - **Export as FHIR® JSON** — share the full structured IPS Bundle for use with clinical systems and FHIR® tools
- **Works Everywhere**
  - **International standard** — produces a conformant IPS 2.0.0 / FHIR® R4 Bundle readable by clinical systems worldwide
